Get in touch

Email sandeep.baliyan@live.in and you'll get a reply from a person, usually within a couple of days. There is no support team — Echo is made by one developer, and that is who answers.

It helps to include your device and iOS version, and what you were doing when something went wrong. If it involves a medicine or a journal entry, please describe it rather than sending a screenshot with your own content in it — we do not need to see your data to help.

Common questions

Where did my entries go after signing in?

Signing in with a different method, or a different email address, creates a separate account with its own data. If you first signed up with an email address and later used Sign in with Apple, and Apple gave you a private relay address, those are two accounts. Sign out and back in using the method you started with. Nothing has been deleted.

Dictation isn't working

Echo needs both microphone and speech-recognition permission. Check Settings → Echo and make sure both are on. Dictation uses Apple's own speech recognition; Echo never receives the audio.

I'm not getting medicine reminders

Check three things, in order: notifications are allowed for Echo in iOS Settings; Med reminders is on in Echo under your account menu → Settings; and the medicine has a scheduled time rather than being marked as-needed. As-needed medicines never generate reminders — that is deliberate, not a fault.

I deleted a medicine by mistake

Deleted medicines stay in Recently deleted at the bottom of the Meds tab for seven days, with everything you had recorded. Tap Restore. After seven days, or if you chose “Delete permanently”, it is gone.

Is the adherence percentage counting something I can't see?

It counts scheduled doses only, over the last 30 days, and a dose counts as on time if it was marked within two hours of when it was due. As-needed medicines are never counted, because there is no appointed time to be late for.

How do I get my data out?

Account menu → Export my data. You get a JSON file with everything Echo holds for you, through the normal share sheet.

How do I delete my account?

Account menu → Delete my account. It is immediate and irreversible, and removes your entries, to-dos, lists, medicines, dose history and scans from our servers.

Privacy

Echo stores health information when you use the Meds feature, and sends photographed prescriptions to an AI provider to read them. All of that is described plainly in the privacy policy.

Reporting something urgent

If you have found a problem affecting the accuracy of medication information — a dose showing the wrong time, a reminder that does not arrive, an adherence figure that looks wrong — please say so in the subject line and it will be looked at first.

Echo tracks what a prescriber wrote. It does not give medical advice, and it is not a substitute for asking a pharmacist or doctor.
